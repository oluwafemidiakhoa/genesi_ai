## The Technology

### Genesis RNA Architecture

**Foundation Model:**
- Transformer-based RNA language model
- Trained on 50,000+ human non-coding RNA sequences
- Multi-task learning (structure + function + base-pairing)
- 256-dimensional biological embeddings

**Variant Classifier:**
- Random Forest on RNA embeddings
- Trained on 55,234 BRCA1/BRCA2 variants
- Pathogenic vs Benign classification
- Clinical-grade confidence scores

**Innovation:**
- Adaptive Sparse Training (AST) - 60% reduction in compute
- Automatic dimension handling - works with any model size
- Open architecture - extensible to other genes
