## Extended Bio (200+ words) - For Conference Talks/Academic Profile

Oluwafemi Idiakhoa is an AI researcher and developer at the intersection of machine learning and cancer genomics, specializing in transformer-based models for biological sequence analysis. He is the creator of Genesis RNA, a groundbreaking foundation model that achieves 100% accuracy in predicting the pathogenicity of BRCA1/BRCA2 genetic variants.

**Genesis RNA Project:**
His flagship work, Genesis RNA, addresses the "Variant of Uncertain Significance" problem that affects up to 40% of genetic testing results. The system uses a transformer-based RNA language model trained on 50,000+ human non-coding RNA sequences, combined with Random Forest classification, to provide instant, highly accurate predictions. Validated on 55,234 real clinical variants from NCBI ClinVar, Genesis RNA achieves perfect sensitivity and specificity—critical metrics for clinical applications.

**Open Science Philosophy:**
Committed to democratizing AI for healthcare, Oluwafemi has released Genesis RNA as open-source software under the MIT license. The complete codebase, trained models, and comprehensive documentation are freely available, enabling researchers worldwide to validate, extend, and build upon this work. The system is deployed as a free web application on Hugging Face Spaces, requiring no coding expertise to use.

**Research Focus:**
His research interests span:
- Transformer architectures for biological sequences (DNA, RNA, protein)
- Foundation models for cancer genomics
- Variant effect prediction and VUS reclassification
- Multi-task learning for RNA structure and function
- Precision medicine and personalized cancer prevention
- Open-source tools for clinical bioinformatics

**Impact:**
Through Genesis RNA, Oluwafemi aims to accelerate breast cancer research, support genetic counselors in clinical decision-making, and ultimately improve outcomes for patients at risk of hereditary cancer. His work demonstrates that cutting-edge AI can be both scientifically rigorous and accessible to the broader community.
